title: European Countries would want to vote on Frontex like Switzerland
description: Meisser Economics financed a survey about how the Swiss and their neighbors preceive democracy in Switzerland and the European Union.
---
<p>
In spring 2022, the Swiss could vote on whether they agree with additional funding of Frontex in order to better secure the Schengen border. We took the opportunity
to ask citizens from all over Europe whether they would also like to be able to vote on this matter and if so, how they would would. Both answers are an overwhelming
YES, with the opinions on Frontex largely reflecting that of the Swiss citizens.
</p>
